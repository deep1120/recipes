    The fudgy brownie test

    Look for set edges and a center that doesn't jiggle. A toothpick should show moist, sticky crumbs, not fluid batter. Cool completely and chill for 1–2 hours before slicing if your batch is too sticky. Chilling does not cook raw batter.

    Eggless S’mores Chocolate Chip Cookies

    Three medium cookies with crispy golden edges, a soft, chewy center, melty chocolate, crunchy graham cracker pieces, and gooey marshmallows. Melted butter helps them spread.
